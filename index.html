Authorized Google Ads Account Management

Create, manage, and monitor Google Ads campaigns from one dashboard.

Neak Sina Ads Manager is a campaign management platform for businesses and marketing teams who want to connect their Google Ads accounts and manage campaigns, budgets, ad groups, ads, assets, and performance reporting from a centralized dashboard.

Access is granted only through Google OAuth 2.0 authorization. The platform does not store Google passwords and does not access any account without explicit user permission.

Google Ads API Usage

How the platform uses the Google Ads API

Neak Sina Ads Manager uses the Google Ads API only to perform actions requested by authorized users for Google Ads accounts where permission has been granted. The platform does not sell Google Ads account data and does not access accounts without explicit user authorization.

01

Create and manage campaign budgets, campaigns, ad groups, ads, and assets.

02

Read campaign and account performance reports for dashboard analytics.

03

Pause, enable, and update campaigns according to user actions.

04

Manage targeting settings and campaign configuration for connected accounts.

Authorization Workflow

User controlled account connection

Step 1

User signs in

The user opens Neak Sina Ads Manager and chooses to connect a Google Ads account.

Step 2

User grants consent

Google OAuth is used for authorization. The platform never asks for a Google password.

Step 3

Account data loads

Only permitted Google Ads accounts are shown in the dashboard.

Step 4

User manages campaigns

Campaign actions and reports are available for the authorized account only.

Step 5

User can disconnect

The user may revoke access from the app or directly through Google Account permissions.

About Us

Built for businesses that run Google Ads

Neak Sina Ads Manager is an advertising technology platform built and operated in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We build campaign management tools for businesses and marketing teams that use Google Ads to reach their customers across Southeast Asia and beyond.

The platform was developed to give authorized Google Ads users a simpler, more controlled interface for managing their own advertising accounts without leaving the security controls of Google's OAuth authorization system.

We are registered as an independent software tool and apply for Google Ads API access to enable our campaign management features. Our developer token application is supported by a full design document, working test implementation, and this business website.
