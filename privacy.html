Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Overview

Neak Sina Ads Manager helps authorized users manage Google Ads campaigns. We collect and process information only as needed to provide account connection, campaign management, reporting, support, security, and compliance.

Information we collect

We may collect account profile information, contact information, user settings, connected Google Ads customer IDs, campaign configuration, reporting metrics, audit logs, support messages, and technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information.

Google Ads data

When a user authorizes access, Neak Sina Ads Manager may read or manage Google Ads account information, campaign budgets, campaigns, ad groups, ads, assets, targeting settings, and performance reports. We use this data only to provide the requested advertising management features for the authorized account.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, connect permitted Google Ads accounts, display campaign dashboards, create and update campaign objects, generate reports, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support or compliance requests.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell Google Ads account data or personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers only when needed to operate the platform, comply with law, protect the service, or fulfill a user request.

Access control and revocation

Users can disconnect their Google Ads account from Neak Sina Ads Manager or revoke access through their Google Account permissions. After revocation, the platform will stop using that authorization to access the account.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed for platform operation, legal obligations, security, dispute resolution, and audit purposes. Users may request deletion of account data by contacting support.

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, restricted credential handling, audit logging, and operational safeguards to protect user and Google Ads account data.
